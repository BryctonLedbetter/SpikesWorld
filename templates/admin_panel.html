📖 How to Run Spike's World

Hey Brycton! This is your control room. 🦎 Anything you change here shows up on the website for everyone!

📣 Post an Announcement

Type a Title and Message, then click Post Announcement. The newest shows on the homepage. Delete old ones anytime.

🌵 Add a Countdown

Counting down to something fun (like a new light arriving)? Type what it is and pick the Date, then click Add Countdown. The soonest one shows big on the homepage and counts down automatically.

🎂 Set Spike's Birthday

Pick Spike's birthday and click Save. The Age Tracking card figures out his age in years, months, and days — plus his growth stage — all by itself!

💧 Update the Tank Condition

Change the Hot side, Cool side, and Humidity boxes, then click Save Tank Condition.

❤️ Update Spike's Health

Put a short Status (like "Perfect") and add Notes, then click Save Health.

👥 Add Caretakers

Type a Name and optional Role, then click Add Caretaker. They appear on the Caretakers card.

🖼️ Upload Photos

Choose a photo, add a caption, and click Upload Photo. Visitors can click any photo to see it bigger!

📡 The Live Camera

The "View Spike Live" card streams your camera automatically — nothing to do here. When the camera's on, the dot turns green and says "Live"!

🔒 Keep your password secret! Only you can log in here. Click Log Out when you're done, especially on a shared computer.
