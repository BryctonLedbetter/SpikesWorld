📖 How to Run Spike's World

Hey Brycton! This is your control room. 🦎 Here's everything you can do — anything you change here shows up on the website for everyone who visits!

📣 Post an Announcement

Got news about Spike? Type a Title and a Message, then click Post Announcement. The newest one shows up on the homepage. You can delete old ones with the Delete button.

💧 Update the Tank Condition

Change the Hot side, Cool side, and Humidity boxes to match Spike's tank, then click Save Tank Condition. You can type it however you like, e.g. "95°F" or "35%".

❤️ Update Spike's Health

Put a short Status (like "Perfect" or "Shedding") and add Notes about how he's doing. Click Save Health and it updates the health card on the homepage.

🖼️ Upload Photos

Click Choose a photo, pick a picture of Spike (jpg, png, gif, or webp, up to 12 MB), add a Caption if you want, then click Upload Photo. Visitors see them in the "Pictures of Spike" gallery. Delete any photo with its Delete button.

📡 The Live Camera

The "View Spike Live" card streams your camera automatically — you don't need to do anything here. When the camera is on, the dot turns green and says "Live"!

🏠 See Your Changes

Click View Site (top right) anytime to see the real website. The homepage updates on its own every 30 seconds, or just refresh the page.

🔒 Keep your password secret! Only you can log in here. When you're done, click Log Out — especially on a shared computer.
